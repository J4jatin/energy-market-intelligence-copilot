Title: Inox Clean Energy acquires Vena Energy India for $627m
Source: Energy Monitor
Published: 2026-08-19
Link: https://www.energymonitor.ai/news/inox-clean-acquires-vena-energy-india-627m/

Inox Clean Energy, a company within the INOXGFL Group, has acquired Vena Energy India in a transaction valued at around Rs60bn ($626.7m).
