Title: Moldova simplifies rules governing solar, heat pumps, storage systems
Source: PV Magazine
Published: 2026-08-24
Link: https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/08/24/moldova-simplifies-rules-governing-solar-heat-pumps-storage-systems/

Legislative changes in Moldova allow solar panels, heat pumps and energy storage systems to be installed without a building permit or urban planning certificate under certain circumstances.
