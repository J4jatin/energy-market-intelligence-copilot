Title: India adds 3 GW of open-access solar in Q2
Source: PV Magazine
Published: 2026-08-24
Link: https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/08/24/india-adds-3-gw-of-open-access-solar-in-q2/

India added a record 3 GW of open-access solar capacity in the second quarter of 2026, taking cumulative installations to 36 GW. First-half additions reached nearly 6 GW, up 42% year on year, driven by strong C&I demand and regulatory deadlines.
