Title: European PPA prices rise 2.3% in July
Source: PV Magazine
Published: 2026-08-24
Link: https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/08/24/european-ppa-prices-rise-2-3-in-july/

European PPA prices rose 2.3% in July, with gains in Great Britain and Italy offset by declines in the Nordics, Spain and Portugal. Contracting remained active, with 24 PPAs totaling 1.1 GW and nine battery storage agreements representing 865 MW.
