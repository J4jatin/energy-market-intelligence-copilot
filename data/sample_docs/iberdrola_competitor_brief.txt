Iberdrola — Competitor Intelligence Brief
(Illustrative sample document for the Market Intelligence Copilot knowledge base)

Company overview
Iberdrola is a Spanish multinational electric utility and one of the world's largest renewable-energy
operators, with major businesses in Spain, the UK (ScottishPower), the US (Avangrid), Brazil, and Mexico.

Strategy and positioning
Iberdrola's strategy is anchored on two pillars: massive investment in regulated electricity networks
and large-scale renewables (onshore wind, offshore wind, and solar). It positions networks as the
backbone enabling electrification and renewable integration.

Networks and renewables
A large share of Iberdrola's capital goes into smart grids and network expansion across its markets,
providing stable regulated returns. In parallel it develops offshore wind in Europe and the US and
utility-scale solar and storage globally.

Green hydrogen
Iberdrola invests in green-hydrogen projects to decarbonise industry, pairing electrolysers with its
renewable generation.

Key competitive takeaways
Iberdrola blends E.ON-style regulated networks with RWE/Ørsted-style renewables at global scale. Its
edge is diversification across geographies and the network-plus-renewables model; its risks are
regulatory changes in key markets and the capital intensity of simultaneous grid and renewables build-out.
