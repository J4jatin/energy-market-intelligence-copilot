Octopus Energy — Competitor Intelligence Brief
(Illustrative sample document for the Market Intelligence Copilot knowledge base)

Company overview
Octopus Energy is a UK-based energy retailer and technology company, one of the fastest-growing energy
suppliers in Europe. It combines renewable electricity supply with a proprietary technology platform.

Strategy and positioning
Octopus differentiates through technology and customer experience rather than generation scale. Its
strategy centres on its Kraken software platform, smart tariffs, and flexibility services that reward
customers for shifting demand to times of abundant renewable power.

Kraken platform
Kraken is Octopus's cloud-based customer and energy-management platform. Octopus licenses Kraken to
other utilities worldwide, making software licensing a significant growth business alongside retail.

Flexibility, EVs, and heat
Octopus offers smart EV-charging and heat-pump tariffs, agile time-of-use pricing, and virtual power
plant services that aggregate distributed assets to balance the grid.

Key competitive takeaways
Octopus competes with E.ON and other retailers in supply, but its real edge is software (Kraken) and
demand flexibility — a different model from asset-heavy generators like RWE. Its main risks are retail
margin volatility and exposure to wholesale price swings.
