Ørsted A/S — Competitor Intelligence Brief
(Illustrative sample document for the Market Intelligence Copilot knowledge base)

Company overview
Ørsted A/S is a Danish energy company and the world's largest developer of offshore wind. Formerly
DONG Energy, it divested its oil and gas business to become a pure-play renewables developer,
majority-owned by the Danish state.

Strategy and positioning
Ørsted's strategy is built almost entirely around offshore wind, complemented by onshore wind, solar,
batteries, and power-to-X (green hydrogen and e-fuels). It targets a large global offshore-wind
capacity across Europe, the US, and Asia-Pacific.

Offshore wind leadership
Ørsted develops and operates major offshore wind farms in the North Sea, Baltic Sea, UK, US East
Coast, and Taiwan. It has faced project write-downs and supply-chain cost inflation in the US market,
which pressured returns and reshaped its investment plans.

Power-to-X and hydrogen
Ørsted invests in green hydrogen and e-fuels, using offshore wind to power electrolysis for industrial
and transport decarbonisation.

Key competitive takeaways
Ørsted is the direct benchmark competitor to RWE, Vattenfall, and EnBW in offshore wind. Its edge is
unmatched offshore development experience; its main risks are supply-chain inflation, rising interest
rates (offshore wind is capital-intensive), and permitting/grid-connection delays.
