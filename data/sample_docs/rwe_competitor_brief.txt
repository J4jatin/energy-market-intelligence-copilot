RWE AG — Competitor Intelligence Brief
(Illustrative sample document for the Market Intelligence Copilot knowledge base)

Company overview
RWE AG, headquartered in Essen, Germany, is one of Europe's largest electricity generators.
Following the 2018 asset swap with E.ON, RWE consolidated most of the two groups' power
generation, including renewables, and became a generation-focused company.

Strategy and positioning
RWE's strategy is built around its "Growing Green" investment programme, targeting a large
build-out of renewable capacity — onshore wind, offshore wind, solar PV, and batteries — plus
flexible backup generation and hydrogen. RWE positions itself as a leading global renewables
developer while phasing out coal.

Offshore wind and renewables
Offshore wind is a core pillar of RWE's growth. It develops and operates offshore wind farms in
the North Sea and Baltic Sea, the UK, and the US East Coast. RWE also expands onshore wind and
utility-scale solar across Europe and North America, and invests in battery storage to firm
intermittent output.

Coal phase-out and hydrogen
RWE is progressively retiring lignite and hard-coal plants in line with Germany's coal-exit
timeline, while investing in hydrogen-ready gas plants intended to provide dispatchable backup
capacity as renewables scale.

Key competitive takeaways
RWE's earnings are more exposed to wholesale power prices and merchant risk than E.ON's
regulated grid model. Its competitive edge is scale and execution capability in offshore wind
development. Vattenfall and Ørsted are its principal offshore-wind competitors in Northern Europe.
