Uniper SE — Competitor Intelligence Brief
(Illustrative sample document for the Market Intelligence Copilot knowledge base)

Company overview
Uniper SE is a German energy company headquartered in Düsseldorf, spun off from E.ON in 2016.
It operates power generation, global energy trading, and gas storage/import infrastructure. After
the 2022 gas crisis it was stabilised through German government support and effectively nationalised.

Strategy and positioning
Uniper is repositioning from a fossil-heavy generator toward a decarbonising portfolio. Its strategy
emphasises security of supply, expanding its gas and LNG infrastructure as a transition bridge, and
investing in green hydrogen, batteries, and flexible generation.

Hydrogen and gas
Uniper operates large gas storage facilities and LNG import capacity (including the Wilhelmshaven
terminal). It is developing green-hydrogen production and hydrogen-ready gas plants, positioning its
storage assets for future hydrogen use.

Renewables and phase-out
Uniper is retiring coal capacity and growing renewables and storage, though from a smaller base than
RWE or Vattenfall. Trading remains a major earnings driver.

Key competitive takeaways
Uniper competes with RWE in generation and trading and overlaps with EnBW and Vattenfall on gas and
flexibility. Its distinct edge is gas/LNG storage and import infrastructure; its main risk is the
speed and cost of decarbonising a historically fossil-heavy fleet under state ownership.
